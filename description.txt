把泰拉瑞亚的寻路、建造、战斗拆成一套工具,并用它们串出一条从新世界开局到打死肉山的完整流程。

寻路核心:走、跳、挖、放平台统一按帧数标价(帧数来自照着源码还原的物理引擎),
每一步把所有动作模拟一遍,挑"落点离目标最近 + 这步最便宜"的那个。贪心跑主路,卡住时 A* 接管。

聊天命令:
  /start  跑完整流程
  /halt   急停,停掉全流程和所有正在跑的动作
  /vis    调试图层开关(录像前关掉)

模组配置里可以开关辅助项(岩浆凝固、常驻 buff)和调试图层

需要配合 DragonLens 使用(关刷怪/无敌/倍速)。

源码与说明:https://github.com/Reisenbug/TerraBlind

---

A toolbox of pathfinding, building and combat primitives for Terraria, wired by hand into one
pipeline that runs from a fresh world all the way to killing the Wall of Flesh.

Pathfinding: walking, jumping, mining and platform-placing are all priced in frames, taken from
a physics engine reconstructed off the game's source. Every step simulates all of them and picks
the one whose landing is closest to the goal for the least cost. Greedy drives the route; A*
takes over the moment it gets stuck.

Chat commands:
  /start  run the whole pipeline
  /halt   emergency stop, kills the pipeline and every running action
  /vis    toggle the debug overlay (turn it off before recording)

Mod config toggles the assists (lava freezing, always-on buffs) and the debug overlay.

Requires DragonLens (disable spawns / godmode / fast-forward).

Source and details: https://github.com/Reisenbug/TerraBlind
